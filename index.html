Final strategy

Official Duel League ranking is evaluated in 4-week blocks. The 7-week view below is our alliance planning horizon, not a separate game season. The alliance saves by default, scouts every opponent, and spends hard only when the matchup gives a realistic chance to convert resources into a weekly win.

Diamond objectives

Minimum objective: avoid a 0–4 season.
Preferred objective: 2 wins in each 4-week block.
Rolling resource target: find roughly 3 favorable win opportunities across 7 planned weeks.
After 2 wins: hard save unless the visible league cutoff says more is required.

Alliance spending rule

Favorable opponent: push.
Even opponent: push only when the season record creates pressure.
Unfavorable opponent: save unless the season is at must-win status.
Actual promotion/demotion cutoff: always overrides the default strategy.

The largest single daily VP value. Do not rely on Saturday to rescue a week if enough earlier VP are already gone.

Decision rules used by the app

Gold: keep pushing while a promotion path remains possible. If a loss removes every path inside the configured promotion cutoff, save for the next cycle.
Diamond with 2 wins: hard save unless the actual cutoff still creates danger.
Diamond with 1 win: save against bad matchups; push a favorable matchup for the preferred second win.
Diamond with 0 wins entering Week 3: pressure rises sharply; push any realistic matchup.
Diamond with 0 wins entering Week 4: must push. A 0–4 season is the outcome the plan is specifically designed to avoid.
Visible league cutoff: if the calculated promotion/demotion line shows that one win is insufficient, the app escalates to a second required win.
